## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a premium, nurturing, and artisanal boutique identity for baby and toddler apparel. It strikes a balance between editorial warmth and dependable modern commerce. The visual atmosphere is calm, soft, and trustworthy—evoking the touch of brushed organic cotton, sunlit nursery corners, and heirloom craftsmanship.

Targeting thoughtful parents, gift givers, and families, the UI avoids hyper-saturated, chaotic juvenile aesthetics in favor of a curated, gentle sophistication. The chosen design movement is **Warm Editorial Minimalism with Tactile Warmth**:
- **Generous Whitespace:** Spacious, breathable layouts that allow photography of soft textures and delicate knits to breathe.
- **Organic Editorial Grace:** Artisanal serif headlines paired with ultra-legible, humanist geometric sans-serif body typography.
- **Tactile Softness:** Subtly rounded geometry, diffused warm ambient shadows, and gentle pastel micro-surfaces that convey safety, care, and quality.
- **Clarity in Commerce:** Uncompromising clarity in sizing chips, pricing displays (₹ INR), delivery guarantees, and purchase funnels.

## Colors

The color palette is anchored in comforting, sun-kissed nursery hues balanced by grounded neutral tones to ensure high readability and contrast across all screens.

- **Primary (`#D9933B` - Honey Amber):** The core call to action and focal point color. Represents warmth, golden sunlight, and premium cheerfulness. Used for primary buttons, active states, key conversion moments, and promotional accents.
- **Secondary (`#E8A598` - Soft Blush):** A tender, comforting petal rose tone used for curated collection tags, heart/wishlist fills, gift packaging highlights, and secondary promotional banners.
- **Tertiary (`#8EA89D` - Calming Sage / Mint):** An organic, grounding botanical green used for eco-friendly badges, organic cotton certifications, stock indicators, and soothing informational callouts.
- **Neutral Dark (`#262320` - Soft Charcoal):** Replaces harsh pure black. Provides deep, warm contrast for high-legibility body copy, headings, and icon outlines without visual fatigue.
- **Background & Canvas (`#FAF7F2` - Warm Cream / Alabaster):** Replaces sterile stark white with a gentle linen cream base, offering an inviting, tactile backdrop for product photography and surface containers (`#FFFFFF` for elevated product cards).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the artisanal elegance of `EB Garamond` with the clean, highly legible geometry of `Manrope`.

- **Headlines (`EB Garamond`):** Brings boutique storytelling, literary grace, and bespoke craftsmanship to category headers, seasonal hero banners, and story titles. Uses relaxed medium-to-semibold weights with subtle negative tracking for larger sizes.
- **Functional UI & Body (`Manrope`):** Delivers clarity and ergonomic ease for shopping flows. The geometric balance and open counters make descriptions, delivery dates, and age parameters effortless to parse on both mobile screens and desktop monitors.
- **Price Displays:** Standardized with `Manrope` in bold weight paired directly with the INR currency symbol (`₹`). Currency symbols match the baseline and optical weight of the numeral string, avoiding reduced superscripts to maintain immediate scanability.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt rhythmic grid structure, combining a centered fixed-max fluid grid container with responsive adaptivity:

- **Desktop (1024px and above):** Max-width 1280px with a 12-column layout. Gutters are fixed at `1.5rem` (24px) with canvas margins starting at `3rem` (48px). Product grids render 4 columns per row.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.25rem` (20px) gutters and `2rem` (32px) margins. Product grids adapt to 3 columns or a 2-column roomy showcase.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1.25rem` (20px) outer screen margins. Product catalogs collapse into a streamlined 2-column staggered or equal-height card grid.

Horizontal rhythm between form fields, cards, and text pairings strictly adheres to the `space-*` scale:
- `space-xs` (4px): Micro-gaps between currency symbols and values; icon-to-label spacing in compact badges.
- `space-sm` (8px): Gaps between age pills, chips, and rating stars.
- `space-md` (16px): Internal padding for standard cards, inputs, and button interiors.
- `space-lg` (24px): Padding for modal containers, drawer slide-outs, and card headers.
- `space-xl` (40px): Section breaks between carousel modules and related categories.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is conveyed through delicate, warm-tinted ambient shadows and tonal surface layering rather than cold gray drop shadows. This preserves the soft, safe, and welcoming feel of the boutique:

- **Canvas Tier (Ground):** `#FAF7F2` (Warm Cream). The foundational background that ties the entire shopping experience together.
- **Surface Tier (Resting):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure Crisp White) applied to product cards, search dropdown sheets, and review panels. Resting cards carry an ultra-diffused warm amber ambient drop shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(138, 92, 45, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(38, 35, 32, 0.03)`.
- **Raised Tier (Hover / Active):** Cards and actionable modules lift subtly on interaction: `0 10px 28px -4px rgba(138, 92, 45, 0.12), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(38, 35, 32, 0.04)`.
- **Overlay Tier (Modals / Cart Drawer / Sticky Menus):** `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(38, 35, 32, 0.14)`, complemented by a translucent frosted backdrop blur (`rgba(250, 247, 242, 0.8)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`).
- **Outlines & Borders:** Low-contrast hairline dividers (`1px solid rgba(217, 147, 59, 0.12)`) establish subtle structural containment without breaking the warm, organic softness.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Rounded (Level 2)** foundation, harmonizing structured architecture with baby-friendly gentle softness:

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Applied to form inputs, search fields, product images, thumbnail galleries, and alerts.
- **Large Radius (`rounded-lg` - `1rem` / 16px):** Applied to elevated product cards, category promo banners, filter drawers, and modal dialogs.
- **Extra Large Radius (`rounded-xl` - `1.5rem` / 24px):** Utilized for boutique hero display cards, curated lookbook frames, and bottom sheets on mobile.
- **Pill Geometry (`full` / 9999px):** Selectively reserved for interactive micro-components, including CTA buttons, age range chips (`0-3M`, `3-6M`), promo pills, and cart item counter badges to provide an inviting, thumb-friendly touch surface.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Full pill contour (`rounded-full`), `#D9933B` background with white text (`#FFFFFF`), `font-weight: 600`. Padding: `0.75rem 1.75rem`. Features an amber-tinted ambient glow on hover (`0 6px 16px rgba(217, 147, 59, 0.3)`).
- **Secondary / Soft Button:** Pill contour, warm cream surface tinted with blush (`#FCEFEA`), text in soft terracotta/neutral (`#7A4237`), zero border. Ideal for secondary actions like "Add to Wishlist" or "Quick View".
- **Outlined Button:** Pill contour, `1.5px` border in `#D9933B`, transparent fill, text `#D9933B`. Transitions to soft amber fill (`rgba(217, 147, 59, 0.08)`) on hover.

### Chips & Age Badges
- **Age Selectors (`0-3M`, `3-6M`, `6-12M`, `1-2Y`, `2-4Y`):** Compact pill shape. In default state, warm alabaster surface (`#F4EFE6`) with `#262320` text. Selected state transitions to solid `#D9933B` background with crisp white typography and a soft shadow.
- **Feature Badges:** Pill-shaped tags using the tertiary sage tone (`#EAF2EE` background with `#436657` text) for "100% Organic Cotton" or "Sensitive Skin Approved", and secondary blush (`#FDF1EE` background with `#8F4C40` text) for "Newborn Essential".

### Product Cards
- **Structure:** Crisp white surface (`#FFFFFF`), `1rem` border radius, subtle warm border (`1px solid rgba(217, 147, 59, 0.1)`).
- **Image Area:** 4:5 vertical aspect ratio with rounded inner top corners, featuring an unobtrusive, floating circular wishlist heart button (`36px × 36px`, white surface with gentle shadow) positioned in the top-right corner.
- **Card Content:** Category label in `label-sm`, title in `title-md` (`EB Garamond` or `Manrope`), age group preview, and clear price hierarchy showcasing current price (`₹X,XXX`) in bold charcoal alongside optional strikethrough original price in muted tone.

### Search Bar
- **Styling:** Pill or `0.75rem` rounded container with a softly tinted surface (`#F5EFEB`). Embedded magnifying glass icon in warm charcoal. Placeholder text styled in warm taupe (`#8C847A`). Active focus reveals a clean border in `#D9933B` with zero harsh browser outlines.

### Navigation & Header Icons
- **Wishlist & Cart Icons:** Clean, modern line weight (1.75px stroke) rendered in `#262320`. Cart icon features a pill notification badge in `#D9933B` with white counter numerals positioned at the top-right corner.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Fields:** Height `48px`, border radius `0.5rem`, `#FFFFFF` surface with `1px solid rgba(38, 35, 32, 0.15)`. Floating labels using `label-md`. Focus ring uses a soft honey glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(217, 147, 59, 0.2)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `20px` touch targets with rounded corners (`4px` for checkboxes, circular for radios). Checked state fills with `#D9933B` containing a crisp white checkmark.
