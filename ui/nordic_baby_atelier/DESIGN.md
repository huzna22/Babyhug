## Brand & Style

The design system articulates an avant-garde Scandinavian boutique aesthetic for modern babywear and nursery essentials. By subverting traditional expectations—moving away from predictable juvenile sweetness into an airy, high-design architectural narrative—the system fuses serene powder blues with subtle whisper-pinks and tailored structured contours. 

The atmosphere evokes tactile minimalism: light-filled Nordic interiors, brushed organic cotton, natural birchwood, and soft sea mist. It speaks to design-conscious parents who appreciate restrained playfulness, functional purity, and tactile restraint.

- **Experimental Soft-Structure:** Crisp, tailored contours and soft-square geometries rather than generic circular pills provide a high-fashion, editorial foundation.
- **Airy Powder Serenity:** Powder blue acts as the structural driver, balanced by warm cream and gentle petal tones to cultivate a tranquil, calming sanctuary.
- **Graphic Playfulness:** Expressive, quirky display grotesque typography delivers personality and warmth, grounded by precision sans-serif body type.

## Colors

The palette establishes an inverted, architectural take on boutique babywear, using calming cool tints alongside warm tactile neutrals:

- **Primary (`#A0C4E2` - Powder Blue):** The atmospheric driver. Employed for structural accents, active borders, brand signatures, primary interactive containers, and key navigational milestones. Deep powder blue (`#4C759A`) provides high-contrast accessible states when required.
- **Secondary (`#F7CAD0` - Whisper Pink):** A muted pastel blush reserved for secondary collection tags, gifting highlights, and curated seasonal badges.
- **Tertiary (`#D66D80` - Rosewood):** A deeper, grounding berry-pink tone used for intentional micro-accents, wishlist active states, and alert indicators.
- **Neutral (`#23282E` - Slate Ink):** A softened charcoal-navy with subtle blue undertones. Replaces harsh black to maintain gentle readability for all body text, outlines, and structural typography.
- **Surface & Backgrounds:** The base canvas relies on `#FAF8F5` (Linen Cream), producing an organic, tactile paper-like warmth, while `#FFFFFF` provides clean elevation for product presentation tiles and utility layers.

## Typography

The typographic language pairs the expressive, sculptured rhythm of `Bricolage Grotesque` with the pristine, modern utility of `Hanken Grotesk`:

- **Display & Headlines (`Bricolage Grotesque`):** Delivers a fresh, contemporary personality. Its quirky character shapes and subtle ink traps introduce gentle warmth and playfulness without lapsing into childish bubble typography.
- **Body & Functional UI (`Hanken Grotesk`):** Provides sharp, clean legibility across compact mobile shopping matrices, filter trays, size selections, and care instructions.
- **Commerce & Numbers:** Sizing specifications, price indicators, and dimensional metrics use `Hanken Grotesk` in medium and semibold weights with tabular alignment for immediate scanning.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored in an airy, architectural grid system designed for uncluttered Scandinavian merchandising:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** Fluid 12-column layout with a maximum container of 1360px. Outer margins sit at `2.5rem` with column gutters at `1.5rem`. Editorial modules and product showcase catalogs span dynamic asymmetrical columns (e.g., 5-column editorial alongside a 7-column product matrix).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Product listings resolve to 3-column rows.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4-column structure with compact `1rem` margins and `0.75rem` gutters. Merchandising lists flow in a structured 2-column format with optimized tap spaces.

Spacing rhythm follows modular increments:
- `space-xs` (4px): Micro-gaps between status dots, price tags, and compact meta badges.
- `space-sm` (8px): Gaps between sizing swatches, variant selector chips, and inline icon pairings.
- `space-md` (16px): Structural internal padding for input controls, notification boxes, and standard buttons.
- `space-lg` (24px): Card interior spacing, flyout drawer padding, and section micro-headers.
- `space-xl` (40px): Breaths of negative space separating curated looks, category banners, and content partitions.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy departs from dense, murky shadows, embracing instead **tonal layering and low-contrast outlines** with faint sky-tinted ambient glows:

- **Surface Ground:** The base platform resides on `#FAF8F5` (Linen Cream).
- **Resting Layer (Level 0):** Product surfaces and editorial panels sit directly on `#FFFFFF` bordered by a crisp, low-contrast hairline contour: `1px solid rgba(160, 196, 226, 0.35)`. No muddy shadows are cast at rest.
- **Floating / Hover Layer (Level 1):** Interactive cards elevate smoothly with an airy, powder-blue tinted ambient spread: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(160, 196, 226, 0.4), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(35, 40, 46, 0.04)`.
- **Overlay Layer (Level 2):** Quick-shop sheets, filter drawers, and modal sheets combine a light border with a subtle elevation veil: `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(35, 40, 46, 0.12)`, supported by a frosted glass backdrop filter (`rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.8)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`).

## Shapes

The design system enforces a **Soft (Level 1)** geometric contour logic. Departing from hyper-round pill forms common in mass juvenile design, this system favors deliberate, architectural soft-squares that evoke tailored modern scandi craft:

- **Base Corner Radius (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Applied to form fields, age/size selector chips, utility tags, thumbnails, and interactive buttons.
- **Medium / Large Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Applied to main product presentation containers, quick-view cards, and promotional callouts.
- **Extra Large Radius (`0.75rem` / 12px):** Reserved for floating bottom navigation sheets, search flyout drawers, and modal dialogs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid powder blue (`#A0C4E2`) with slate ink text (`#23282E`), `font-weight: 600`, radius `0.25rem`, padding `0.75rem 1.5rem`. On hover, transitions to a deeper pastel blue (`#8EB7DA`) with a soft blue ambient glow.
- **Secondary Action:** Powder blue wash surface (`#E3F0F9`) with slate ink text (`#23282E`) and subtle hairline border (`1px solid #B9D5EB`). 
- **Subtle / Ghost Action:** Transparent background, `1px solid rgba(160, 196, 226, 0.6)` outline, transitioning to `#E3F0F9` on hover.

### Chips & Sizing Selectors
- **Size / Age Swatches (`NB`, `0-3M`, `3-6M`, `6-12M`):** Tailored soft-square geometry (`0.25rem` radius). Resting state is crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) with a `1px solid rgba(160, 196, 226, 0.4)` border and `#23282E` text. Selected state adopts a full powder blue fill (`#A0C4E2`) with slate ink typography and a distinct `1px solid #4C759A` stroke.
- **Attribute Badges:** Delicate rectangular badges with `0.25rem` radius. "Organic Cotton" renders in powder blue tint (`#E3F0F9` with `#4C759A` text); "Gift Edit" renders in petal pink wash (`#FDF0F2` with `#D66D80` text).

### Product Cards
- **Construction:** Crisp white canvas (`#FFFFFF`), `0.5rem` corner radius, encased in a delicate `1px solid rgba(160, 196, 226, 0.35)` border.
- **Imagery:** 4:5 vertical proportion with gentle `0.25rem` inner radius. Wishlist heart icon sits top-right as a tailored square button (`32px × 32px`, `0.25rem` radius) on semi-translucent cream.
- **Meta Block:** Category in `label-sm` (Slate Ink at 60% opacity), product title in `title-md` (`Hanken Grotesk`), and price in `label-lg` with high contrast.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- **Fields:** Height `44px`, `0.25rem` corner radius, `#FFFFFF` interior with `1px solid rgba(35, 40, 46, 0.16)`. Placeholder copy in muted slate (`rgba(35, 40, 46, 0.45)`). Focus rings avoid browser defaults, applying a 2px crisp `#A0C4E2` stroke.
- **Search Bar:** Integrated horizontal bar with soft cream-white fill, slate search glyph, and trailing filter trigger.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkboxes:** Crisp square (`0.25rem` radius), `18px × 18px`. Checked state fills `#A0C4E2` with a `#23282E` checkmark glyph.
- **Radio Buttons:** Concentric circular targets (`18px`). Selected state features a `#23282E` active center pip surrounded by a `#A0C4E2` ring.

### Floating Cart & Flyouts
- Soft-square counters in `#A0C4E2` with `#23282E` bold numerical indicators. Sliding drawers utilize `#FAF8F5` backing and `12px` frosted blur overlays.
